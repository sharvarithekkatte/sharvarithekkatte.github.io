Case study 02

Polo Ralph Lauren “The Polo Bear Takeover” — Campaign Concept

A case study exploring how Polo Ralph Lauren could reach Gen Z beyond social media. I developed a five-phase out-of-home (OOH) campaign using the iconic Polo Bear to drive personalization, real-world experiences, and year-round brand engagement.

“Your Bear.
Your Story.”

“An independent creative exploration in May 2025 that sparked my interest in marketing.”

The Polo Bear Takeover — plansbook

Challenge

1 in 6 Gen Z consumers surveyed had abandoned their favorite social media platform because of excessive advertising. How could Polo Ralph Lauren connect with Gen Z beyond the feed?

Insight

Gen Z values experiences, personalization, and community. 83% view shopping as an experience, 75% value personalized experiences, and 54% favor brands that foster community.

Big idea

Gen Z already connects with the Polo Bear through viral outfit-recreation videos. The opportunity was to turn that existing connection into deeper engagement through personalization, self-expression, and storytelling. Gen Z already dresses like the Bear. Now, they can personalize it to reflect their own style and story.
